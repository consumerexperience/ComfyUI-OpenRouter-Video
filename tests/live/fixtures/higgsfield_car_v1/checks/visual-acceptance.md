# Human visual acceptance — Golden Smoke 01

Review the generated clip by eye. These are qualitative checks, not a score or automated vision verdict.

- [ ] Car remains recognizably the same vehicle as the reference.
- [ ] Wheel and body geometry stay stable.
- [ ] Motion direction is coherent and forward.
- [ ] Camera remains mechanically plausible and locked relative to the car.
- [ ] Bodywork does not melt or morph.
- [ ] Reflections remain temporally coherent.
- [ ] No spontaneous cut or transition occurs.
- [ ] Output feels commercially usable and pleasant.
