This model is hosted by one provider. OpenRouter forwards every request to it directly — no routing decisions to make.

Performance

Throughput is how fast the model writes (tokens per second — higher is better). Latency is total round-trip time (lower is better). TTFT is time-to-first-token — how long before you see anything appear (lower is better).

Uptime

Uptime is the percentage of the past 3 days that at least one provider was responding to requests. Availability is the percentage of time that inference was successfully served. OpenRouter continuously monitors and uses the next-best provider when one returns an error.

Seedance 2.0 Mini is a video generation model from ByteDance. It supports text-to-video, image-to-video with first and last frame control, and multimodal reference-to-video with image, video, and audio inputs. It supports 480p and 720p output for 4-15 second videos. The number of tokens is given by (height of output video * width of output video * duration * 24) / 1024

Seedance 2.0 Mini costs from $0.03363/second at 480p to $0.6804/second at 4K for Video (with audio), from $0.03363/second at 480p to $0.6804/second at 4K for Video (no audio), from $0.02018/second at 480p to $0.4082/second at 4K for Video (with video input), $3.50/M tokens for Video Tokens (with audio), $3.50/M tokens for Video Tokens (no audio), and $2.10/M tokens for Video Tokens (with video input).

Seedance 2.0 Mini generates video at 480p and 720p, supports 4–15 second clips, covers the 21:9, 16:9, 4:3, 1:1, 3:4, and 9:16 aspect ratios, can be steered with a first frame and last frame image, and produces a matching audio track.

Seedance 2.0 Mini accepts text, images, video, and audio as input and returns video.
